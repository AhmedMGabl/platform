## 🎯 **BEST PRACTICES**

### **Command Style**
- **Be specific** - "Create task to fix login bug" vs "Fix that thing"
- **Include context** - "Assign to Sarah in the marketing project"
- **Set deadlines** - "Due by Friday 5 PM"
- **Prioritize** - "Make it high priority"

### **Getting Better Results**
- **Provide context** - Tell AI about your projects and team
- **Use natural language** - Speak normally, don't use commands
- **Give feedback** - Tell AI when it does well or poorly
- **Trust the AI** - Let it be proactive and autonomous

### **Automation Tips**
- **Start small** - Automate simple tasks first
- **Build incrementally** - Add complexity gradually
- **Test thoroughly** - Verify workflows work as expected
- **Monitor performance** - Track AI accuracy and efficiency
